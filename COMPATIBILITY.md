## Status levels

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Playable | Played into normal gameplay with no known problems |
| Minor issues | Playable with small graphics or sound glitches |
| Major issues | Starts, but crashes, hangs or is too broken to play |
| Boots, looks right | Reaches the title or the first scenes; not played further |
| Doesn't boot | Black screen or hang before the title |

## Reporting

Open an issue with:

- the game's name, region and serial (e.g. SCUS-94163),
- the core version,
- what happened and how far you got,
- a photo or video if it's a graphics problem.
